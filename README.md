# BOB — Dryer Vent Squad booking assistant

Customer chat (`/chat`, embeddable with `/embed.js`) and office dashboard (`/admin`) for Dryer Vent Squad West Valley.

- **Booking**: question-by-question flow, exact prices from the service catalog, contact-code verification, held times, and a finished booking is a scheduled Jobber job with a visit.
- **Rules**: service-area ZIP gate, travel fee, drive time under 20 minutes between jobs, route scoring from the scheduling workbook, optional same-day scheduling, named-approver step only for emergency same-day service. Other legacy approval-only exceptions/overflow remain office-only.
- **Smart chat**: Gemini (or OpenAI) reads typed messages into BOB's commands and answers approved FAQs. Then BOB's *voice* writes the opening of the reply to what the customer actually said (acknowledging, answering, reassuring), and the rules engine's required message — the next question, an offer, the price summary, safety steps — always follows unchanged. The AI can't set prices, skip required questions, or book; anything it writes passes the price/promise guard, and any failure falls back to the engine's own reply. Buttons, safety reports and the contact/verify/time/approval steps never go through the voice. See `server/chat.mjs` (`withVoice`) and `server/assistant.mjs` (`speak`, `normalizeVoice`).
- **Savings estimate**: when loads take about 2 cycles (or 3+), the package offer shows a small tile with what a clear vent could save (dollars a year, hours of drying a week), from the extra cycles and published averages (`SAVINGS_AVERAGES` in `shared/conversation.mjs`: 5 loads a week, 45-minute cycles, 2.5 kWh per cycle, about 15¢ per kWh in Arizona). Gas dryers get the time only. The AI knows the same figures.
- **"I don't know" answers** are fine: BOB says it'll make a note, the technician double-checks on site, and the Jobber job notes list those items under "Tech to double-check on site".
- **Emergency booking**: an urgent clog offers Emergency Same-Day alongside Premium 1-2-3 and Ultimate Protection. Same-day depends on availability: after the customer picks a time, the team checks whether a technician can get there today (named approval), while the customer sees an animated waiting screen with progress updates and can cancel the request. If the team declines, the request times out, or the customer cancels, BOB goes back to the choices (regular packages only after a decline or timeout). A picked package can be canceled from the summary with "Cancel package".
- **Booking report (Custom-AIs)**: every confirmed ChatDaddy booking emails bookings@custom-ais.com with the customer, the job and its value, and running totals for the week (Monday start), month and year in Arizona time: booking number, revenue, and commission at $12 per appointment (`server/booking-report.mjs`). Office-entered appointments aren't counted. It uses the SendGrid sender and is sent at most once per booking. Optional Vercel settings: `BOOKING_REPORT_EMAIL` (another address, or `off`) and `BOOKING_COMMISSION_CENTS` (default `1200`). The Master Admin can send a sample from Settings → Connections ("Send test report") to check it arrives; it books nothing.
- **Service area map**: Settings → Service area draws Google Maps (Map and Satellite) under the editing tools when `GOOGLE_MAPS_BROWSER_KEY` is set in Vercel. That's a separate browser key restricted to the dashboard's web address with only the Maps JavaScript API enabled; the server's `GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY` is never sent to the browser. Without the browser key, or if Google rejects it, the map shows OpenStreetMap and says why.
- **After booking**: a confirmed booking shows a 20-second countdown on the receipt with a "Stay open" button (paused while the card is saved). Then the widget closes on the website, or the standalone page returns to dryerventsquadaz.com. It doesn't run for a restored receipt, a booking waiting on the office, or the dashboard preview.
- **Upsell before picking a time**: when prices are approved, the add-ons step shows promo cards: the DryerFlex hose upgrade ($79, regular $99), the sanitizing fog treatment ($79), $20 off when both are added (taken off the fog line), and for Premium the Ultimate Protection upgrade (which includes DryerFlex). DryerFlex isn't offered when the package already includes it or there's no dryer. Prices come from Services & prices; the $99 regular price and the $20 deal are in `shared/catalog.mjs`.
- **Progress while booking**: loading openings (first load, More times, Check day) shows a popup that checks off "Checking the schedule for openings…, Calculating routes…, Assigning a technician…, Gathering optional alternatives…"; confirming shows "Creating your account…, Assigning your technician…, Adding your visit to the schedule…, Sending your confirmation…". Both say "Please do not navigate away from this screen" and stay up at least about 2.5 seconds. A tapped time outlines and fills green while it's held, then turns solid green ("Held for you"); Confirm & book fills green while booking and ends on "Confirmed".
- **Saved confirmation**: "Save confirmation" saves a designed PNG appointment card (`src/customer/appointmentCard.ts`), with the share sheet on iPhone.
- **Messages**: Explicit Twilio Verify SMS/email and independently gated appointment texts are documented in `TWILIO-SETUP.md`. LeadConnector is removed; confirmation email uses separately configured SendGrid transactional delivery.
- **Early availability checks**: same-day emergency inquiries notify the configured approver (or Ryan's configured technician phone) during chat, before the booking form. The office inquiry does not reserve or confirm a visit. Requests received at or after 2 PM Arizona time are not eligible for same-day emergency service.
- **Editable service area map**: Settings → Service area map supports measured Google Routes driving miles or a circular radius. Move the base pin, drag radius handles, adjust mileage sliders, draw included/excluded polygons, and drag their vertices to customize coverage. Save settings to apply those boundaries to customer address checks; exclusions take priority. Existing ZIP coverage stays active until the edited map rules are saved. Road-routing failures stay unverified in driving-distance mode.
- **Activity**: the private BOB activity tab stores chat messages, widget actions, availability inquiries, provider acceptance/message IDs and failures in Postgres. Records survive conversation expiration. Provider acceptance does not establish handset delivery. Activity records use the existing events table and are excluded from webhook dispatch.
- **Minimize**: the customer header can collapse and restore BOB without resetting the conversation. The embedded widget also closes its outer panel and restores the same iframe.

## Run

```sh
npm install
npm run build && node server/index.mjs   # http://127.0.0.1:3000 (in-memory database unless env vars are set)
npm test
```

## Deploy (Vercel)

Vite build to `dist/`, one function at `api/bob.mjs`. Copy `.env.example` for the variables to set.
The database is the `bob` schema in Supabase (apply with `npm run db:schema`, which prints the SQL), reached with `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` + `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`. Jobber tokens are stored in `public.chatdaddy_jobber_tokens` (`supabase/chatdaddy_jobber_tokens.sql`).
Jobber redirect URI: `<app url>/api/jobber/callback`.

### Optional Jobber Client Hub link

In Settings → Connections, the Client Hub setting accepts only the public company login link copied from **the connected company's Jobber Settings → Client hub**. Confirm that the displayed Jobber company is the company whose link you copied. Jobber account identity can be verified through OAuth; ownership of a manually supplied Hub URL is **not** independently verified by the API.

The link is optional and blank by default. When safely bound to the same connected account as a newly booked appointment, it appears as an **Open Client Hub** button and plain-text URL in the booking-confirmation email. It is not included in four-digit verification messages or SMS confirmations. It is a login page, not a personalized authentication token or an appointment-verification action.

Disconnecting or changing the Jobber connection disables the binding until an administrator reconfirms it; ordinary OAuth token refreshes do not. If account identity cannot be checked, or a historical appointment lacks the verified account binding, the email is sent without the link. Invalid links, personal login tokens and arbitrary query parameters are rejected. No emails are resent merely by editing this setting.

### Confirmation channels and editable templates

Settings → Templates selects customer confirmation **Text**, **Email**, **Both**, or **Off** using the existing channel settings. Existing installations retain their saved choices. Selecting a channel does not configure its transport or bypass customer texting consent; unavailable channels are reported as skipped rather than silently rerouted. Owner booking alerts keep their separate toggle/recipient and emergency approvals and four-digit verification messages are unchanged.

Edit plain-text customer SMS, email subject/body, and optional owner-booking SMS. Insert only the listed `{{variable_name}}` placeholders. Sample previews use fictional details and send nothing. Blank/reset fields retain built-in defaults. Unknown or malformed variables, oversized templates, and email-subject line breaks are rejected by the server. Email HTML escapes all custom text; no scripts or custom HTML execute. Arizona dates/times and USD totals are formatted from the actual appointment. `{{client_hub_url}}` is email-body-only and blank unless the connected-account binding is valid. A custom email body replaces the built-in body, so include that variable if you want its Client Hub link.

The customer SMS STOP footer is added outside editable copy. Customer confirmations use permanent per-appointment, per-channel attempt claims: retries and uncertain responses cannot produce automatic duplicate sends. Provider acceptance does not prove delivery. Legacy SMS attempt records also suppress resend. Owner-alert claims remain separate. Changing a template does not resend previous notifications.

### HTML email mode and connection checks

Settings → Templates offers Plain text (the existing default) or HTML for the custom confirmation-email body. HTML is parsed and sanitized on the server using maintained `sanitize-html` and `htmlparser2` packages. Basic text, tables, lists, HTTPS/mailto/tel links and a narrow set of inline formatting styles are supported. Scripts, forms, frames, SVG/MathML, remote images, event handlers and network-loading CSS are removed. Variables are allowed in text; only the account-bound `{{client_hub_url}}` may occupy an entire link `href`. Pasted Client Hub links are rejected in HTML links. Plaintext alternatives are generated from sanitized content. Blank custom bodies keep the built-in default email.

Use **Preview HTML safely** for an explicit, authenticated preview with fictional data; no email is sent. The preview is sanitized before appearing in an inert, sandboxed, network-blocked frame. Editing the body or mode clears stale previews. Unsupported formatting may be removed. HTML must retain readable text after sanitizing.

Connections distinguishes **Configured**, **Setup required**, **Off**, authorization connection and recent API operation status. Green means an actual recent successful operation/read check, not merely a key being present. SMS/email acceptance is explicitly not delivery confirmation. Operation records expire after 15 minutes and are bound to current configuration/provider credentials; historical successes are not invented for requests before tracking was installed. Optional Jobber/Twilio account-name lookups are separate from sending permissions and do not mark message delivery broken when a limited key cannot read account metadata.

**Refresh connection status** checks database access and reads permitted connected-account names, with server rate limits and a cross-instance cooldown. It reloads recent recorded Verify, SMS, email, AI and Maps operation results; it never automatically sends a test message or makes a paid AI/Maps test request. Configure links open the relevant provider/server dashboard; secrets remain server-side and no credentials are edited through this page.
